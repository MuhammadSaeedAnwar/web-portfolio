Project 01 · Auto repair

MotorForge Auto Care

Website + booking + customer portal + admin

Everything an independent repair shop runs online. Customers book a slot, then follow their car through the shop: received, inspection, waiting on approval, ready to collect. The front desk works from one dashboard.

  • Booking with real time slots, so two people can't take the same one
  • Customer accounts with vehicles, estimates and service history
  • Admin screen for bookings, estimates, customers and repair status
  • Emails for confirmations and status changes

Slate & Ridge Roofing

One-page site for quote requests

Built for one job: get a homeowner with a roof problem to send their details. The quote form is the first thing on the page, and every service section links back to it.

  • Form asks "what's wrong with the roof", not "which service"
  • Three steps that explain exactly what happens after you ask
  • Area list and FAQ for the usual questions before someone calls
  • Tap-to-call for leaks that can't wait
